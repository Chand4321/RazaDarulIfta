Our Heritage

Preserving the Sacred Sciences

Light of Knowledge

Founded with the vision to provide authentic, moderate, and well-researched Islamic legal opinions (Fatwa), Raza Darul Ifta Wal Qaza stands as a sanctuary for those seeking clarity in an ever-changing world.

Our methodology is rooted in the classical Ahlus Sunnah Wal Jama'ah tradition, specifically adhering to the Hanafi school of jurisprudence while respecting the diversity of the four valid Madhahib.

Our Mission

To provide Shariah-compliant solutions that protect the Iman of Muslims from modern deviations.

Our Vision

To establish the supremacy of Islamic law and lead the Ummah toward spiritual success.

Our Pillars of Ifta

Attaining the Appeasement of Allah Subhan wa Ta'aala

01

Superiority of Religion

We firmly believe in the superiority of Deen in every aspect of life, ensuring that worldly affairs are governed by the sacred light of Shariah.

02

Guarding the Ummah

We are dedicated to rescuing Muslims from the deviancy caused by internal and external fitnah, preserving the authentic path of our predecessors.

03

Classical Rulings

Every Fatwa is backed by rigorous references from classical texts like Al-Hidayah and Fatawa Hindiyyah, led by the Maslak of Ahlus Sunnah.
