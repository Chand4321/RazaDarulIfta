Raza Darul Ifta Wal Qaza

Under Guidance: Hazrat Rahil Rehmani Raza Sahab

Ref: RD-ZKT-1447

Date: 28 Ramadan 1447

Istifta (Question):

"Regarding the common practice of 'Heavy Deposits' for rooms: Is Zakat obligatory upon the amount paid by the tenant to the homeowner?"

Al-Jawab (The Verdict):

In the name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.

In the case mentioned, Zakat is Wajib (obligatory) on the Heavy Deposit.

It is established in the authoritative books of Fiqh that the lender (creditor) remains the owner of the amount given as a Qarz (loan). Consequently, the responsibility for Zakat lies with the lender.

It is evident that the amount provided as a 'Heavy Deposit' is effectively a Qarz given to the homeowner by the tenant.

In conclusion: If the tenant who provided the deposit is a Malik-e-Nisab (possessor of the minimum wealth threshold), Zakat is Wajib upon them for this amount, not upon the homeowner (debtor). For further details, refer to Bahar-e-Shariat.

And Allah Almighty Knows Best.
