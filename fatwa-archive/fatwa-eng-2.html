Raza Darul Ifta Wal Qaza

Under Guidance: Hazrat Rahil Rehmani Raza Sahab

Ref: RID-SLH-1447

Date: 25 Ramadan 1447

Istifta (Question):

"What do the scholars say regarding a situation where the Imam is on the upper level/roof and the followers (muqtadis) are below the roof? Will the Salah be valid in this case?"

— Petitioner: Ali Hasan Nizami

Al-Jawab (The Verdict):

In the scenario mentioned, the Salah will be valid. However, performing Salah on the roof of the Masjid without a Shari'i necessity (Dharurah) is prohibited and Makruh (disliked).

Therefore, the Imam should lead the prayer on the ground floor. When the space below is full, other congregants may move to the upper level, as a Shari'i necessity would then exist.

Aala Hazrat Imam Ahmed Raza Khan Qadri Barelwi (may Allah have mercy upon him), citing Fatawa Alamgiri, states:

”الصعود علی سطح کل مسجد مکروہ ولھذا اذا اشدت الحر یکرہ ان یصلوا بالجماعۃ فوقہ“

"Ascending the roof of any Masjid is Makruh; hence, when the heat is intense, it is Makruh to pray in congregation upon it."
(Fatawa Ridawiyyah, Vol 1, p. 232)

In short, the Salah will be valid, but due to the Karahat (dislike), there will be a reduction in the reward (Thawab). Therefore, one should avoid praying on the Masjid roof needlessly.

And Allah Almighty Knows Best.

Mufti Muhammad Iqbal Raza Khan Huzoori
