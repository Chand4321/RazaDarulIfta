Raza Darul Ifta Wal Qaza

Under Guidance: Hazrat Rahil Rehmani Raza Sahab

Ref: RD-8842

Date: 12/02/2026

The Inquiry:

"Is it permissible to use perfumes containing synthetic alcohol, and does it affect the validity of Salah according to the Hanafi Madhhab?"
الجواب وبالله التوفيق

The Verdict:

In the name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.

It is permissible to use perfumes containing synthetic or denatured alcohol (derived from sources other than grapes or dates). Such substances are not considered Najasat-e-Ghaliza (heavy impurity). Consequently:

  • The body and clothes remain pure for prayer.
  • The Salah performed while wearing such perfume is valid.
  • Wudu is not nullified by the application of perfume.

Sources:

Fatawa Shami (Radd al-Muhtar), Fatawa Hindiyyah Vol. 5, Research of Muta'akhireen.

والله أعلم بالصواب

Hazrat Rahil Rehmani Raza Sahab
